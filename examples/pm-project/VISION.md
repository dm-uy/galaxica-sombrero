Develop one public, harness-neutral methodology in full detail. Cover shared concepts, roles, flow, skills, prompts, templates and checks; retain runtime mechanisms as declared adaptations. It serves research, product, operations and software work, with execution methods supplied separately by runtimes.

## Remaining purpose and evidence boundaries

Four process stubs remain: `process/git.md`, `process/code-review.md`, `process/publication.md` and `process/review.md`. Wider uninspected F4/F5/F8 duties, detailed memory/learning transfer, applicable-source/unique-duty/delta accounting, operational adoption and publication remain later or unresolved. An interface, card, form, fixture, parser or adapter does not prove complete original-source transfer or runtime enforcement. Source-level readiness and actual original bounded executions do not establish global installation, detached distribution, model effectiveness, comparable savings or reduced owner coordination effort.

Lightweight recording of discovered errors in existing notes remains the practice. Systematic learning, improvement experiments and comparative measurement are deferred. Preserve accepted, held and failed source history without converting it into a new grant or resetting budgets. The historical API/database → frontend → Telegram → MCP route does not activate a new consumer exercise; M25C needs its separate decision.

The immediate outcome is a reviewable, independently checked source candidate. Whole common/package regressions, privacy and link closure, real CLI/flow/maps, semantic retention and exact artifact identity are required before result acceptance. At this preparation baseline these transformed-export checks are NOT_RUN. Main integration, publication and external installation remain separately authorized actions.
